# BYFIT Gym Rubber Flooring — Evidence-Based Email Templates

Follow [discovery before quotation](../../references/discovery_before_quotation.md). These are original BYFIT structures, not quotations from a course. New buyer/project first reply: no prices, ranges, starting prices, discounts, priced catalogues or quotation attachments, even if the buyer asks. Later price delivery requires a passing quote-gate. Do not use fixed Day 0/3/5 schedules.

Read [current seller facts](seller_facts.json) first. Sizes do not confirm every finish/thickness combination. If asked for HS, preserve `400821000` exactly as the seller-provided reference, with complete declaration format and applicability still to be confirmed. Do not pad digits or use it for equipment/rolls.

## 1. First substantive reply — non-price value

Use only unanswered questions; retain the buyer's original subject/thread. Attach a relevant non-price catalogue only if it actually exists and is available for this task. Never say “attached” without attaching it. No invented certifications, capacity, delivery promises or sample terms.

### Variant A: Focused discovery

Hi [Name],

Our rubber tile sizes are 50 × 50 cm and 100 × 100 cm.

For a training area, a finish or nominal thickness alone does not establish suitability. The actual loads, surface construction and installation conditions need to be assessed together.

Will weights be dropped directly onto the tiles, and what loads should we assess? Is this replacing flooring with an existing problem, or a new fit-out? What must a sample demonstrate for your team to approve it?

These details will help us assess the suitable configuration and a useful comparison for your project.

## 4. Email Group next touch — use only when relevant

- Selection evidence: add a real model-specific document that resolves an expressed concern.
- Sample/acceptance: agree checks, sample scope and only authorized costs after the pricing gate.
- Negotiation: compare equivalent scope; concessions need seller approval.
- Silence: ask about the last unresolved decision and add genuinely useful information; no price bait.
- Order: confirm final configuration, PI version and operational details without assuming acceptance.

Buyer reply interrupts the planned sequence. Respect refusal, postponement and stop requests. No automatic sending or cross-channel pursuit.
