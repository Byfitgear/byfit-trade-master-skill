# BYFIT Gym Rubber Flooring — WeChat / WhatsApp Templates

Follow [discovery before quotation](../../references/discovery_before_quotation.md). Same gate across channels: no pricing in a new buyer/project's first substantive response. Moving an email lead to WhatsApp or WeChat does not reset or bypass the gate. These are drafts, not authorization to contact anyone.

Read [current seller facts](seller_facts.json) first. Sizes do not confirm every finish/thickness combination. If asked for HS, preserve `400821000` exactly as the seller-provided reference, with complete declaration format and applicability still to be confirmed. Do not pad digits or use it for equipment/rolls.

## 1. New inquiry — useful first response

Hi [Name], Eric from BYFIT here. Our rubber tile sizes are 50 × 50 cm and 100 × 100 cm. For a training area, a finish or nominal thickness alone does not establish suitability. The actual loads, surface construction and installation conditions need to be assessed together. [Ask 2–3 material unanswered questions, or only one if that is the sole gap.]

Keep the actual version concise. Do not repeat the buyer's quantity/country/thickness, or claim a catalogue or photo is attached when absent.

## 2. First message asks “best price”

Hi [Name], Our rubber tile sizes are 50 × 50 cm and 100 × 100 cm. To select the right option for [confirmed application], [one relevant decision point with its reason]. Is this replacing flooring with an existing problem, or a new fit-out? What must a sample demonstrate for your team to approve it?

No unit price, price range, discount, starting price, or priced attachment. Acknowledge the request naturally without reciting the internal no-first-quote rule. If the buyer already answered either question, replace it with the most material remaining gap.

## 3. Further discovery

Thanks, that clarifies [confirmed fact]. [Supported implication for configuration or acceptance.] Could you confirm [one remaining material condition]?

Buyer statements can establish needs; website inferences alone cannot. An explicit new-project goal can replace an existing pain point.

## 4. Later authorized quote — only after gate passes

Hi [Name], based on [confirmed use/priority/objective], the suitable option is [seller-confirmed configuration]. The approved offer is [currency/amount/unit/Incoterm/place] for [quantity]. [Confirmed scope, limitation and validity.] Would you like to [one relevant next decision]?

Run quote-gate first. Do not use this block in the first substantive reply. Do not invent price, freight, discounts, approvals or promised arrival dates.

## 5. Sample / order / after-sales

- Sample: confirm exact model and acceptance checks; price and shipping only after the gate and seller approval. No automatic “free” or “refundable” promise.
- Order: report actual milestone and source; no made-up progress percentage or guaranteed shipping.
- After-sales: acknowledge observed issue, request only evidence needed to diagnose, and follow confirmed warranty/remedy terms.
- Silence: refer to one unresolved decision; avoid simultaneous messages on multiple platforms.

Use the language and channel requested. Retain confirmed earlier terms; no automatic sends, added contacts or public buyer logging.
