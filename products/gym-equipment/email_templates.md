# BYFIT Gym Equipment — Evidence-Based Email Templates

Follow [discovery before quotation](../../references/discovery_before_quotation.md). These are original BYFIT structures, not quotations from a course. New buyer/project first reply: no prices, ranges, starting prices, discounts, priced catalogues or quotation attachments, even if the buyer asks. Later price delivery requires a passing quote-gate. Do not use fixed Day 0/3/5 schedules.

Do not reuse flooring sizes/HS. Frame thickness, cable ratings, certifications, model range, warranty, service coverage and availability need current model-specific seller evidence. Overall 20 production lines does not mean 20 dedicated equipment lines.

## 1. First substantive reply — non-price value

Use only unanswered questions; retain the buyer's original subject/thread. Attach a relevant non-price catalogue only if it actually exists and is available for this task. Never say “attached” without attaching it. No invented certifications, capacity, delivery promises or sample terms.

### Variant A: Focused discovery

Hi [Name],

I'm Eric from BYFIT in China; we work with B2B wholesale buyers on gym equipment.

For a commercial equipment selection, the user group, peak usage and available operating space are more useful starting points than appearance alone. We should compare the actual model and service requirements, not assume similar-looking machines are equivalent.

Which users and training activities must the equipment support? Are you solving a current issue such as downtime or limited space, or fitting out a new facility? What are the essential acceptance criteria for your trainer or purchasing team?

These details will help us assess the suitable configuration and a useful comparison for your project.

Best regards,
Eric | BYFIT
sales@sdbyfit.com

## 2. Discovery follow-up — not yet price-ready

Hi [Name],

Thanks for clarifying [confirmed use/priority]. That means [supported selection implication, not an invented product guarantee].

The remaining point is [specific gap]. [One question whose answer changes the selection or acceptance check.]

Eric | BYFIT

Record the buyer's own answer and source. If there is no previous defect, confirm the purchasing objective instead; do not manufacture a pain point.

## 3. Needs-matched quotation — subsequent reply only

Entry condition: first substantive reply completed; buyer-confirmed application, priorities, scope, decision basis and pain/objective; seller-confirmed suitable model; authorized matching offer. Run `python3 scripts/trade_tools.py quote-gate <private-input.json>`. A table with price placeholders is NOT a permitted first email.

Hi [Name],

## 4. Email Group next touch — use only when relevant

- Selection evidence: add a real model-specific document that resolves an expressed concern.
- Sample/acceptance: agree checks, sample scope and only authorized costs after the pricing gate.
- Negotiation: compare equivalent scope; concessions need seller approval.
- Silence: ask about the last unresolved decision and add genuinely useful information; no price bait.
- Order: confirm final configuration, PI version and operational details without assuming acceptance.

Buyer reply interrupts the planned sequence. Respect refusal, postponement and stop requests. No automatic sending or cross-channel pursuit.
